# AI-Assisted Food–Compound Curation

## Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates my experience in food–compound data curation using AI-assisted methods combined with scientific literature review and evidence-based validation.

My professional experience includes curating relationships between foods and bioactive compounds and organizing nutrition-related information into structured datasets.

AI tools were used to support the curation process, including identifying potential food–compound relationships, assisting with literature screening, and improving the efficiency of data review.

## AI-Assisted Curation

AI was used as a supporting tool for:

- Food–compound relationship identification
- Literature and evidence screening
- Compound–food mapping
- Identification of potential scientific sources
- Data organization and normalization
- Preliminary classification of evidence

AI-generated suggestions were reviewed and validated against scientific literature and appropriate reference sources before being considered for the curated dataset.

## Curation Focus

The project covers:

- Food–compound mapping
- Bioactive compound curation
- Indian food composition information
- Scientific literature review
- Evidence assessment
- Food and compound name normalization
- Structured nutrition data organization
- Data quality checking

## Evidence & Sources

The curation approach prioritizes reliable scientific and Indian nutrition sources, including:

- Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT)
- ICMR–NIN resources
- FSSAI resources
- Indian peer-reviewed scientific literature
- Other relevant scientific publications
